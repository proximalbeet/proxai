- Best fit: an AI agent, chatbot or automation for a small business; Python scripts and integrations;
  Android or small web apps; n8n/Zapier-style workflows.

  piece for selling AI agents to businesses.

  doing someone's homework or exams, security clearance, relocation, senior full-time roles that
  need 5+ years.
- Flag (don't skip) pay that looks under about $20/hour.
